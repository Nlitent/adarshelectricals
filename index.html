Our Legacy

Adarsh Electricals: The Backbone of Silvassa's Power Distribution

Formed right in the heart of Silvassa—one of Western India's most intensive manufacturing zones—Adarsh Electricals was established to fulfill a critical requirement: providing industries, builders, and factories with highly stable, structurally sound, and custom-tailored low-tension (LT) panel systems.

Over the years, we have built a stellar reputation among regional factories for our rigorous quality protocols, premium engineering specifications, and local after-sales assistance. We adhere strictly to national engineering standards, and every single panel leaving our assembly floor undergoes testing for insulation compliance, voltage fluctuations, and continuous load durability.

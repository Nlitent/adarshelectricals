Blog!

Founded in 2021 and based in Silvassa, Adarsh Electrical & Electronics is a provider of comprehensive power and electrical solutions. The company specializes in the manufacturing of LT Panels and Distribution Boards, focusing on power generation, distribution, control, and automation. Driven by core values of quality, innovation, customer focus, and integrity, Adarsh serves a diverse range of industrial and commercial clients. Their product portfolio includes Main LT Panels, MCC & PCC Panels, AMF Panels, DG Synchronizing Panels, Fire Panels, Heater Control Panels, and Load Banks.

Our Commitment To Exellence

Since our inception in 2021, we have been driven by a single mission: to be the preferred partner for our customers by consistently exceeding their expectations. Operating from our manufacturing facility in Amli, Silvassa, we bring world-class quality to the heart of India's industrial sector.


Industrial Panels (MCC & PCC)

These are the workhorses of the manufacturing world. Motor Control Centres (MCC) and Power Control Centres (PCC) are engineered to handle heavy loads, providing essential protection against faults like overloads, phase imbalances, and voltage fluctuations.

Indoor Panels

For commercial buildings, we provide specialized Main LT Panels and Lighting Distribution Boards designed for reliability in controlled environments. These panels ensure seamless power distribution while maintaining safety and efficiency across large indoor spaces.

Outdoor Panels

Many of our installations, such as Feeder Pillars, are designed to withstand the demands of outdoor placement in townships and large sites, providing critical control over the flow of power.


Custom Panels

Our custom panels are tailored to meet the unique requirements of our clients, offering solutions that are not only efficient but also scalable for future growth. Whether it's a specialized control panel or a custom-designed distribution board, we work closely with our clients to ensure that every solution is perfectly aligned with their operational needs.
